Home

My research focuses on the syntax of natural language and its interface with semantics and pragmatics. I work primarily on Magahi and Hindi, South Asian languages, exploring how their grammatical systems inform broader questions in linguistic theory. The topics I work on include honorification in verbal and nominal domains, split constructions, bare nominals, classifiers, number morphology, and discourse particles.

I am also interested in applying formal linguistics knowledge to natural language processing.

I received my PhD in 2020 from Rutgers University, New Brunswick, USA. I joined IIT Delhi in 2023 as an assistant professor in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences.

My current projects:

  • Honorification in Hindi and the discourse particle ji
  • The syntax of honorific mismatches in Magahi and beyond
  • Bare nominals, classifiers and number morphology in Magahi and beyond
  • Negation in natural language
